# EDWARD — The White Rose Chronicle

Experimental art direction, study 01. This is a visual language, not the studio name.

The governing image is a manuscript page about power exercised through people, letters and seals. Flattened space, expressive ink silhouettes and imperfect gold draw from fifteenth-century English and Burgundian illumination. No cinematic lighting, fantasy armour or theatrical oversized crowns.

The campaign opens and changes chapter on a black text screen, keeping the player's attention on the title, present political condition and next question. That screen carries no illustration yet. Proposed chapter artwork, alongside an audit of every current card vignette, is mapped in ART_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md.

Palette: vellum #eadcc0, ink #24251f, madder #793b37, lapis #344c65, gold #b79a60. Madder identifies the principal title action; thin gold rules frame authority. Do not place decision text over illustrations. Keep figures legible at phone width and illustrations proportional without cropping hands or faces.

The first plate appears unchanged at the title and in Art & credits. Reign and chapter openings use a full-screen paged reading room. The People index now pairs a compact political network with distinct portrait miniatures for 22 named characters. Each portrait draws on late medieval English and Burgundian manuscript painting: tempera colours, vellum, gold, heraldic dress and individual status markers. They are imaginative character studies, not verified likenesses. Portrait assets are cropped and compressed into individual WebP files under `assets/art/portraits/` so cards and the People index load only the relevant image. No company splash branding has been chosen.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: first key artwork for EDWARD, an experimental political role-playing game about Edward IV. Create a distinctive, beautifully crafted illuminated-chronicle illustration, landscape 3:2 composition, no typography. A young clean-shaven Edward IV with shoulder-length chestnut hair, a restrained late medieval open crown and rich red and dark blue robes sits at a modest raised seat in an English royal chamber circa 1461. Three councillors in plausible fifteenth-century robes stand at a table presenting a folded letter and a wax-sealed parchment; hands and expressions communicate competing claims, not combat. Gothic window, timber roof, a tiny distant view of English fields. Artistic language: flattened perspective and carefully drawn silhouettes of English and Burgundian manuscript illumination, expressive angular ink outlines, matte egg-tempera colour, delicate crosshatching, natural warm vellum visible between shapes, restrained imperfect gold-leaf accents. Palette tied to the existing game: parchment ivory, soot black, deep madder red, muted lapis blue, olive green and antique gold. Ornamental perimeter of delicate stems and one small white rose, thin rather than heavy. Main subject and table inside central two thirds, breathable composition usable on a phone. This is an original modern artistic interpretation, not a claimed authentic likeness or reproduction. Avoid photorealism, cinematic lighting, fantasy armour, giant spiky crowns, modern furniture, glowing magic, parchment text, lettering, logos and watermarks.
