# Recorded piano and manuscript art — experimental increment

Music now defaults to seven self-hosted acoustic Yamaha C5 recordings from Alexander Holm's Salamander Grand Piano, CC BY 3.0. Turn Music on by tapping it; change sound and volume under Music & atmosphere. Loading happens only when needed. Original synthesised strings remain selectable. This supersedes the earlier stewardship release's note about the absence of recorded piano.

The score is an original scheduled arrangement whose phrases, tempo and spacing follow the current scene and unrest. It uses recorded individual piano notes, not a recording of a complete pianist performance. This modern interpretive soundtrack is not a reconstruction of fifteenth-century music. Audio preference is separate from world saves. Total MP3 payload is about 571 KiB; the browser decodes the bank in memory.

The first key artwork remains on the title screen. The start of a reign and six later campaign periods now open with a black text interlude: a short situation, one political question, and optional historical context. The interludes are generated from the current year and change no event order, conditions, choices, consequences or saves. Chapter illustrations are future work. The full card-to-vignette inventory and the conceptual gaps it reveals are in ART_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md; the style prompt and asset paths are in ART_DIRECTION.md.

Validation: built JavaScript parses; test_piano.js checks load, cache, pitch, stop, cancellation, fallback selection and retry with mocked Web Audio; all seven MP3s decode without ffmpeg errors. test_campaign.js checks all six year frames, one-time opening transitions and unchanged world state. test_scene_map.js checks the 96 authored card assignments plus 55 dynamic illustrations, including known misclassifications. test_guidance.js confirms existing guide and ending interfaces remain read-only where intended. This is not a rendered iPhone Safari test or a human listening assessment. The Python home-screen browser test could not run because Playwright is not installed. No new 1000-run simulation claim is made for this audiovisual/presentation increment; prior balance results remain in reign_1000_audit.json.
