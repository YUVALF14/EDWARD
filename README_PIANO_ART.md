# Recorded piano and manuscript art — experimental increment

Music now defaults to seven acoustic Yamaha C5 recordings from Alexander Holm's Salamander Grand Piano, CC BY 3.0, layered with recorded folk harp, a synthesized bass-viol line, recorder and a soft frame drum. Turn Music on by tapping it; change sound and volume under Music & atmosphere. Loading happens only when needed. Original synthesised strings remain selectable. The player can mute the frame drum independently. Its denser battle pattern and quieter succession pulse follow the existing mood and tension values, without touching simulation randomness. The harp falls back to a synthesized pluck when recordings are unavailable.

The score is an original scheduled arrangement whose phrases, tempo and spacing follow the current scene and unrest. It uses recorded individual piano notes, not a recording of a complete pianist performance. This modern interpretive soundtrack is not a reconstruction of fifteenth-century music. Audio preference is separate from world saves. Total MP3 payload is about 571 KiB; the browser decodes the bank in memory.

The first key artwork remains on the title screen. The start of a reign and six later campaign periods now open with a black text interlude: short reading pages carrying the situation, political question and historical context, with Back / Next controls and a page count. The interludes are generated from the current year and change no event order, conditions, choices, consequences or saves. Chapter illustrations are future work. The full card-to-vignette inventory and the conceptual gaps it reveals are in ART_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md; the style prompt and asset paths are in ART_DIRECTION.md.
