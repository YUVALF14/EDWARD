CC0 text: VCSL-LICENSE.txt (same CC0 1.0 legal instrument).
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Recordings were converted to mono 32 kHz, mixed into three short cues,
level limited, gently faded and encoded as MP3. Exact archive SHA-256,
source file names and mixing times are in war-sources.json.
Rebuild with python tools/build_war_audio.py (NumPy and FFmpeg).
These are modern recorded foley interpretations, not recordings of historical
battles. No synthetic music, generated voices or modern gunfire is included.
