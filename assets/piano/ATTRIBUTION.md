# Salamander Grand Piano recordings

Creator: Alexander Holm. Instrument: Yamaha C5 acoustic grand piano.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source and original README: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander

C2.mp3, Fs2.mp3, C3.mp3, Fs3.mp3, C4.mp3, Fs4.mp3 and C5.mp3 are unmodified MP3 files from that distribution. See manifest.json for file hashes. EDWARD maps their pitches, shapes envelopes and adds room reverberation at playback. The arrangement is generated by EDWARD; it is not an existing recorded performance. Only seven samples are installed, not the full multi-velocity sample library. Attribution is also available in Music & atmosphere.
